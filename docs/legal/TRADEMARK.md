# Infantry AI Trademark Policy

> **DRAFT. Not legal advice. Not in effect.** The owner makes this official at the public launch, after a name search
> and a lawyer's review.

"Infantry AI", the Infantry AI logo and the emblem (together, the "Marks") are trademarks of SuperiorSoft LLC. The
source code is open (MPL-2.0 for the core, Apache-2.0 for the rest), but neither license grants any right to the
Marks (Apache-2.0 section 6, MPL-2.0 section 2.3). This policy says what you may do. In short: say what is true,
do not suggest we made or endorse your thing, and rename your fork.

## Always fine, no permission needed

- Factual references: "a plugin for Infantry AI", "works with Infantry AI", "built on the Infantry AI plugin API",
  "I use Infantry AI".
- Articles, reviews, tutorials, talks and videos about the software, using the name in plain text.
- Linking to the project, and unmodified screenshots with attribution.
- Naming a plugin so that the name says what it works with, as long as the Marks are not the first or main word of
  the plugin's name: "Kanban for Infantry AI" is fine; "Infantry AI Pro Kanban" is not.
- Distributing an **unmodified** official build, with the Marks as shipped.

## Needs written permission

- Using the Marks in the name of a product, company, domain, app-store listing, package or social account.
- Using the logo or emblem on anything except as in the list above.
- **Modified builds**: a build with changes to the core, or a different plugin set, may not be called "Infantry AI"
  or carry the logo without permission.
- **Hosted services** that offer Infantry AI to others under the name, or in a way that looks like our service.
- Merchandise, events, training or certification using the Marks.
- Any use that suggests we sponsor, endorse or are affiliated with you.

## Forks

You may fork and redistribute the code under its licenses. A fork must use a **different name**, remove the logo and
emblem, and not imply it is the official project. Say "a fork of Infantry AI" in plain text. You do not need to
rewrite internal package names, but your user-facing name, icons and installer must be your own.

## Official plugins

The `Infantry_` prefix and the label "official" mean "published by SuperiorSoft LLC and signed with its key".
Third parties must not use the prefix or claim the label (see [Licensing](../wiki/licensing.md), section 4).

## Rules for permitted use

Use the Marks as proper nouns, do not alter the logo, do not combine it with other marks, and do not use it in a way
that is misleading, disparaging or unlawful. We may withdraw permission for a use that breaks these rules. Where a
nominative use is allowed by law, this policy does not narrow it.
